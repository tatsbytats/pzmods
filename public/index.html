      From Workshop to your server

      Pick your mods. Search by name or import a collection. Check build tags and each mod’s required items on Steam.

      Review Mod IDs. Expand a selected mod to edit its IDs or choose variants. Choose your server build for the correct export format.

      Paste into Hostinger. Copy the two values into WorkshopItems and Mods. Save your settings, then restart your server.

      Map mods can also need a Map setting. This tool doesn’t check conflicts, add dependencies, or change your server.
